# Employment Hero — Product & Pricing Breakdown

> [!info] Billing Notes
> All figures are in **AUD, excluding GST** where applicable. Core HR and Payroll plans carry a **10-user minimum floor** (you pay whichever is higher: the 10-user minimum or your actual billable headcount).

# Employment Hero — SDR Roleplay Battlecard

| Product / Pillar | Core Capability | Cost (AUD ex. GST) | Minimum / Billing Metric | Target Buyer & Size | Pain Points Solved | Key Upsell / Cross-Sell Hook |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core HR (Essentials / Engage / Elite)** | Employee records, leave, onboarding, policies; **Engage** adds ATS, performance & expenses; **Elite** adds analytics & SSO. | **$11** (Essentials) / **$15** (Engage) / **Custom** (Elite) | 10-user min ($110–$150/mo); billed per user/mo | Founders, HR & Ops (10–250+ staff) | Spreadsheet admin, messy onboarding, compliance risk | **HR Advisory** ($15/user, $300 min) for Fair Work legal cover |
| **Payroll & Workforce Mgmt** | Unlimited pay runs, STP, award interpretation; **Rostering & T&A** add-on; **Managed Payroll** (outsourced). | **$10** (Payroll) + **$5** (Rostering) / **$21** (Managed) | 10-user min ($100 Payroll / $50 Rostering); 20-user min ($420 Managed) | Finance, Payroll & Ops (shift-based or growing SMBs) | Double-data entry, manual timesheets, award underpayment risk | **Employment Unlimited** (custom bundle combining HR Elite + Payroll + Advisory) |
| **Recruitment Pro (AI Recruiting)** | Standalone automated job posting, applicant scoring, and 24/7 AI video interviews. | **$199** (50) / **$359** (100) / **$639** (200) / mo | Flat monthly tier by interview volume (no HR base needed) | Talent Acquisition & Hiring Managers (high-volume roles) | Slow screening bottlenecks, high agency fees, no-show interviews | Wedge into **HR Engage** once they want seamless onboarding post-hire |
| **HeroForce (Global / Local EOR)** | Hire full-time staff or contractors across 150+ countries without setting up local legal entities. | **Custom quote** | Flat monthly fee per contractor or %/flat fee per EOR hire (1-user min) | Execs, Legal & Finance scaling remote/global teams | Costly entity setup, international tax & local labour law compliance | Consolidate domestic + global headcount under one HRIS |
| **Swag Work App & Earned Wage Access** | Employee super-app for payslips/leave, retail cashback perks, and **On-Demand Pay (EWA)**. | **$0** to employer (Employees pay **1.3%–1.5%** EWA fee) | Included free with HR/Payroll; EWA capped at 50% / $1,000/wk | HR & CFOs (especially retail, hospitality & blue-collar) | High frontline turnover, financial stress, zero-budget perk requests | Free retention lever to close deals against legacy HRIS competitors |



Objections:

- too expensive, minimum users is 10
	- Handle: a fair work underpayment audit or a unfair dismissal claim cost can cost much more than the $300 per month that you'll pay for the Core HR software 

- We dont trust AI screening
	- Handle: It's more of a screener than a hirer, it decides who's vetted based on your own parameters and let's you focus on intereview cnadidates who are actually qualified for the job.
