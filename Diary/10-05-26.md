2. What did you learn today?

Today I learned about the economic principle of "The Tragedy of the Commons." It occurs when individuals overuse a shared resource for their own personal gain, ultimately depleting that resource. Instead of contributing to its replenishment, people use the resource until there is nothing left. I discovered this when researching OpenClaw in the context of everyone usinf it to "get rich quickly". Someone online pointed out that if evreryone continued to use the opensource library of Openclaw without contributing to it, it would eventually be unusable. Typing this though, I believe they used this economic principle in the wrong way, nevertheless, I learned a new economic principle today.
