## What does a policy cover?

- **INSOLVENCY**
    
    - Liquidation
        
    - Voluntary Administration or Deed of Company Arrangement
        
    - Receivership
        
- **PROTRACTED DEFAULT**
    
    - Evidence that a valid debt exist to the satisfaction of the insurer within the Protracted Default Period.
        
    - Collections is part of the policy, use all means to chase before a claim is paid.
        
- **POLITICAL RISK**
    
    - Inconvertibility;
        
    - Contract Frustration, Contract Cancellation, Export Restriction or Import Restriction (collectively referred to as Insured Perils)
        
    - Public Buyer Default.


## OVERVIEW OF KEY BENEFITS

### Helps protect from bad debts

- Identifies and endeavours to avoid high risks
    
- Helps reduce unexpected losses
    
- Greater insight into customers' likelihood to default
    

### Enhances working capital

- Facilitates access to improved financing
    
- Balance sheet engineering
    
- Additional banking lines

### Embeds credit management disciplines

- Helps enable companies to extend credit terms
    
- Reinforces credit management
    
- Access to credit risk expertise, analysis, & buyer information from the Insurer for existing and new customers
    

### Helps grow your business

- Promotes sales growth whilst maintaining credit management controls
    
- Directs and supports sales to higher margin markets
    
- Supports mergers and acquisitions
