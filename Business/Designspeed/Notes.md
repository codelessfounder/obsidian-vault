"Compulsory income contributions" refers to a legal obligation under the _Bankruptcy Act 1966_ requiring a bankrupt person to pay a portion of their post-bankruptcy earnings back to their bankruptcy estate if their net (after-tax) income goes above a government-set limit.

- **How it works:** It is **not** a 100% wage seizure, and the trustee does not take everything. Instead, the Australian Financial Security Authority (AFSA) sets a base after-tax income threshold (which is **$75,475.40 per year** for someone with zero dependents).
    
- **The 50% Rule:** If a person earns _above_ that threshold, they are required to pay **50 cents for every dollar earned over the limit** to the trustee, who then distributes it to creditors.
